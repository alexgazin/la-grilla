# Apple HIG review — 15 November 2026

Reviewed the current official Apple documentation, not a presumed annual color trend:

| Area | Reference | Implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typography | [Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography) | Apple's iOS guidance gives 17 pt default and 11 pt minimum. Remove 8–10 pt interface labels; use regular/medium/semibold weights, readable hierarchy and the equivalent large-text editing controls. |
| Color | [Color](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color) | Own brand palette, consistent role/action meanings, light/dark AA tokens and a stronger outline in increased contrast. |
| Materials | [Materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) | Real Liquid Glass is confined to navigation on iOS 26. Content surfaces remain quiet. iOS 18 and Reduce Transparency use the opaque adaptive surface. |
| Buttons | [Buttons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/buttons) | Explicit press feedback, prominent actions at least 48 pt, standard icon/navigation targets at least 44 pt, reachable primary actions and adequate spacing. Precision musical canvases also provide the accessible note-list route. |
| Accessibility | [Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility) | Named controls, selection state, Dynamic Type alternatives, Reduce Motion/Transparency and native functional/audit checks. Physical/manual VoiceOver acceptance remains open. |

The concept board establishes a direction. Native captures and interaction checks establish whether its implementation actually works; neither substitutes for the installed release candidate's physical acceptance.
