Turn a small musical idea into a song on your iPhone.

WRITE YOUR HARMONY
Arrange chords into sections. Explore 65 chord types, custom interval formulas, voicings and reharmonization. Compare the original with a proposal, then apply the changes you choose.

BUILD YOUR ARRANGEMENT
Move, copy and resize clips with snap. Reorder sections with their music, zoom into the grid and edit individual clips. Keep the beat playing while you change the arrangement.

FIND A RHYTHM
Play 16 drum pads or use the step editor. Record with count-in, quantize, overdub and Undo. Add a bass line that follows your harmony.

SHAPE YOUR MELODY
Edit notes, timing, length and velocity in the piano roll or note list. Record a single hummed melody over your beat and review its detected notes before applying them. Keep the original voice recording with the project.

PERFORM YOUR SECTIONS
Launch sections on the next bar, repeat a fragment, jump beats, and shape the sound with a master filter and delay. Record a finite performance take, replay it and export it.

CONNECT AND EXPORT
Use compatible USB or Bluetooth MIDI controllers, choose a MIDI destination and map controller knobs. Export MIDI, stereo WAV, aligned stems or a portable project package. Share files through the iOS share sheet.

WORK ON YOUR IPHONE
Projects, recordings and music processing stay on your device. No account, ads or analytics SDK. The Assistant understands supported Russian and English commands for tempo, key, chord progressions, full song forms and jazz harmony. Optional on-device AI interprets free-form descriptions on eligible iPhones with iOS 26 and Apple Intelligence, when its model and selected language are supported. Commands remain available without that model. Optional dictation requires on-device speech recognition support for the selected language.

Designed for iPhone, with light and dark appearances and English and Russian interfaces. Up to 8 tracks, 8 sections and 64 bars per song. Original instrument sounds are synthesized by the app. Voice conversion is monophonic and benefits from a quiet room and headphones; always review the result. MIDI exports contain musical events; external instruments determine their own sound.
