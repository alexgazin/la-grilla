Turn a small musical idea into a song on your iPhone.

WRITE YOUR HARMONY
Arrange chords into sections. Explore 65 chord types, custom interval formulas, voicings and reharmonization. Compare the original with a proposal, then apply the changes you choose.

BUILD YOUR ARRANGEMENT
See the whole song in a visual section map. Move verse, chorus and bridge cards by their handles. Zoom into the multitrack grid to copy, move or resize clips while the loop plays.

FIND A RHYTHM
Play 16 drum pads or use the step editor. Record with count-in, quantize, overdub and Undo. Add a bass line that follows your harmony.

CHOOSE YOUR SOUND
Play recorded acoustic piano and nylon guitar, or find and assign an instrument from an imported SoundFont bank. Load Pocket Kit or reuse your own WAV and AIFF samples across projects. WAV and stems retain your assigned sounds.

SHAPE YOUR MELODY
Edit notes, timing, length and velocity in the piano roll or note list. Record a single hummed melody over your beat and review its detected notes before applying them. Keep the original voice recording with the project.

PERFORM YOUR SECTIONS
Launch sections on the next bar, repeat a fragment, jump beats, and shape the sound with a master filter and delay. Record a performance take and create a new editable song from the played sections and parts. Replay the take or export its live mix.

EDIT IN YOUR OWN WORDS
Describe a change to the open song: “remove the kick in the first two bars”, “add a fill before the second chorus”, “make the hats busier in the last chorus”. The app shows the parts and bars affected, plays before and after from the same place and applies the edit in one step. Compare Boom bap, West Coast and Atmospheric chillhop palettes on your own part at matched loudness.

CONNECT AND EXPORT
Use compatible USB or Bluetooth MIDI controllers, choose a MIDI destination and map controller knobs. Export MIDI, stereo WAV, aligned stems or a portable project package. Share files through the iOS share sheet.

WORK ON YOUR IPHONE
Projects, recordings and music processing stay on your device. No account, ads or analytics SDK. The Assistant understands supported Russian and English commands for tempo, key, chord progressions, full song forms and jazz harmony. Optional on-device AI interprets free-form descriptions on eligible iPhones with iOS 26 and Apple Intelligence, when its model and selected language are supported. Commands remain available without that model. Optional dictation requires on-device speech recognition support for the selected language.

Designed for iPhone, with light and dark appearances and English and Russian interfaces. Up to 8 tracks, 8 sections and 64 bars per song. Voice conversion is monophonic and benefits from a quiet room and headphones; always review the result. MIDI exports contain musical events; external instruments determine their own sound.
