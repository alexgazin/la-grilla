# La Grilla privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026. Applies to the iPhone app published from this repository by alexgazin.

La Grilla does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking identifiers or developer-operated server. No third-party SDK receives your projects or recordings.

Projects, musical notes, controller mappings and voice recordings are stored in the app's local container. iOS device backups may include these files according to your Apple settings. Removing the app deletes its local container; separately exported files and backups follow the storage provider's rules. Delete a project in Projects to remove its local files. Portable project exports contain the original voice recordings saved with that project; choose whom you share them with.

Microphone access starts only after you choose to record a melody or dictate a command. Melody recordings are analyzed on the device. Command dictation requires Apple's on-device speech recognition and is unavailable when the selected language's local recognition is unsupported. La Grilla does not fall back to server speech recognition. You can type commands without granting microphone or speech permissions. Manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Bluetooth permission supports the MIDI device picker you open. Connected MIDI devices supply musical events and receive events only when you select an output destination. The app does not use Bluetooth to track location. Local instrument audio and effects are processed on your iPhone.

The system share sheet and Files picker send or import files only at your request. Once a file is shared, the recipient or selected provider controls its copy. Opening support or repository links uses your browser; GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that website. Please do not post private recordings or projects in a public support issue.

Appearance settings are stored with UserDefaults. System uptime is used only to align audio and MIDI event times and measure elapsed intervals. These values are not sent off the device or used for fingerprinting.

For privacy questions or deletion help, [open a support issue](https://github.com/alexgazin/la-grilla/issues). This policy will be updated before introducing a feature that changes data handling.
